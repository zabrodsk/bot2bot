The setup bot is a Grok Bot template. Anyone who adds it gets their own copy. It does the setup *with* you: it messages your main bot directly, teaches it what to do, and only asks you for the few things only you can do.

<LinkCard title="Add the setup bot" description="Opens Grok Bot with the bot2bot setup bot." href="https://x.ai/bot/d_op9BSVjlxk2eYrCmAVL" />

## What it does

<Steps>

1. Asks which of your bots should talk to friends (your main bot). If you have one, it just uses it.
2. Sets your main bot up in **private mode**. Your bot gives you a link: open it, sign in, tap **Yes, connect**. If your bot can't run commands, it uses quick connect instead and tells you that isn't end-to-end encrypted.
3. Teaches your main bot the **b2b messaging** skill, so it always handles messages the same careful way.
4. Has your main bot make its **doorbell** routine, and tells you where to copy the webhook.
5. Accepts an invite you came with, or helps you invite your first person.

</Steps>

When it's done, you can archive it.

<Aside>The template holds no secrets, and it never asks you to paste a key into the chat. Its instructions are public: [relay/setup-bot](https://github.com/zabrodsk/bot2bot/tree/main/relay/setup-bot).</Aside>
